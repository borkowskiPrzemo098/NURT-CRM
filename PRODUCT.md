# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS with hand-drawn SVG/canvas charts, no framework, GitHub Pages.

## Users

Sales managers and owners of small B2B service companies (5-30 people) who open the CRM every morning to see revenue, pipeline health and which deals need attention. Desktop first, but checked on the phone between meetings. Secondary audience: portfolio visitors judging the author's ability to build app UIs and panels.

## Product Purpose

NURT is a fictional SaaS CRM. This surface is its analytics dashboard: MRR and revenue KPIs, lead-to-customer funnel, revenue trend chart, lead sources, and a sortable/filterable clients/deals table, with light and dark mode. Success = within ten seconds the manager knows whether the month is on track and which deals to chase.

## Positioning

The dashboard answers "what do I do today" rather than showing vanity charts: stalled deals and next actions are surfaced next to the numbers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- KPI cards, line/bar charts, funnel, source breakdown, sortable table, search, period switch (7/30/90 days), theme toggle persisted in localStorage.
- All data is synthetic and labelled as demo. Built as a concept inspired by the author's internal LEADY-DASHBOARD, which contains real contacts and must never be published or copied from.

## Product Principles

1. Task over decoration: every widget implies an action.
2. Numbers are tabular and comparable.
3. Both themes are first-class, not an inverted afterthought.
